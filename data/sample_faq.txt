COMPANY FAQ

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid leave per calendar year.

2. Working Hours
The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.

4. Probation
New employees have a probation period of three months.

5. Learning Budget
Eligible employees receive an annual learning budget of 15000 INR.

6. IT Support
Employees can contact the IT support team by emailing itsupport@example.com.
